# Getting Support

- **Questions and help:** ask in
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/uw-ssec/roap/discussions).
- **Bugs and feature requests:** open an
  [issue](https://github.com/uw-ssec/roap/issues/new/choose) using one of the
  templates.
- **Security concerns:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Before asking, check the [README](README.md), including its Troubleshooting
section, and [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for development setup.
